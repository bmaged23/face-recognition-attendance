MODIFIED DERIVATIVES OF THE NICTA CHOKEPOINT DATASET
These MP4 files were generated from original camera-2 JPEG frames at 30 fps.
Missing source frames repeat the previous frame. Audio is absent.
Annotated outputs additionally contain automatic face boxes and test aliases.
Original frames and archives are retained. Noncommercial experimentation only.
Retain SOURCE_AND_LICENSE.html with copies and derivatives.
Source: https://arma.sourceforge.net/chokepoint/
NICTA; Y. Wong, S. Chen, S. Mau, C. Sanderson, B.C. Lovell,
Patch-based Probabilistic Image Quality Assessment for Face Selection and
Improved Video-based Face Recognition, CVPR Workshops, pp. 81-88, 2011.
